Agrochemical Industry

Agrochemical Reactors

Corrosion-resistant reactors for pesticide, herbicide, and fertilizer intermediate production

Home > Industries > Agrochemical Reactors

Agrochemical media are among the most corrosive in industrial processing — chlorinated solvents, strong acids, high-temperature intermediates. A reactor that fails mid-batch doesn't just cost the batch. It costs days of cleanup, regulatory reporting, and lost production time. We build reactors that survive your medium, not just your specifications.

Industry Challenges

What Makes This Industry Different

Corrosion from chlorinated media

Chlorinated solvents and intermediates attack standard stainless steel, especially at elevated temperatures. Stress corrosion cracking is a real failure mode.

Slurry and suspension handling

Many agrochemical reactions involve solid-liquid suspensions. Standard agitators can't keep solids in suspension or handle abrasive slurries.

Batch purity requirements

Multiple product lines on the same equipment require thorough cleaning. Residue carryover between batches contaminates products and violates regulatory limits.

Our Approach

How ZW Solves These Challenges

Corrosion-Resistant Materials

Hastelloy C276/C22 for chloride service. Titanium for oxidizing acids. PTFE-lined reactors for extreme corrosion. We specify the right material, not the cheapest.

Specialty Agitators

Helical ribbon for high-viscosity slurries. Anchor with scrapers for crystallization. Custom designs for your specific medium properties.

Clean-Changeover Design

Quick-opening heads, top-access manholes, CIP-compatible internals. Reduce batch changeover time from hours to minutes.
