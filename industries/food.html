Food Industry

Food & Beverage Reactors

Sanitary reactors for food, beverage, and flavor production — built for cleanliness and consistency

Home > Industries > Food & Beverage Reactors

Food processing has one non-negotiable requirement: every batch must be clean. A reactor with poor surface finish harbors bacteria. A design with dead zones creates contamination risk between batches. We build reactors where cleaning is not an afterthought — it's engineered in from the first drawing.

Industry Challenges

What Makes This Industry Different

Bacterial contamination

Surface roughness above specification allows biofilm formation. Once established, biofilm is nearly impossible to remove and contaminates every subsequent batch.

Batch-to-batch variability

Inconsistent mixing or heat transfer leads to product variation. Flavor, color, and texture shift between batches — unacceptable in food manufacturing.

Cleaning validation

CIP systems must prove they clean every surface. Poor agitator design or awkward nozzle placement leaves residues that pass visual inspection but fail swab tests.

Our Approach

How ZW Solves These Challenges

Sanitary Finish Standard

Interior polished to ≤0.4μm Ra. All welds ground and passivated. No crevices, no threads inside the vessel — only sanitary fittings and seamless connections.

CIP-Validated Design

Spray ball coverage mapped to vessel geometry. Agitator profile chosen to avoid shadow zones. Drain outlets positioned for complete emptying.

Process Consistency

Temperature control and mixing optimized for your specific medium. Consistent heat transfer and shear rate means consistent product quality, batch after batch.
