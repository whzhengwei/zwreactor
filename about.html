Who We Are

WEIHAI ZHENGWEI MACHINERY CO.,LTD was founded in 2004. For over 20 years we have built a complete product range around one core technology — magnetic coupling (seal-free) agitator drives — serving the chemical, pharmaceutical, food, dyeing, materials, and biochemical industries.

Our facility in Zaobu Industrial Park, Gushan, Weihai (Shandong Province) covers over 20,000 square meters of production space with CNC rolling machines, automated welding equipment, and a full quality inspection lab. We are a licensed Class I & II pressure vessel manufacturer (license obtained in 2013), and ISO 9001 certified.

Some suppliers try to be everything to everyone — valves, pumps, instruments, piping, reactors. We chose to go deep instead of wide. Our team knows reactors. We know what happens inside them, what goes wrong, and how to prevent it. That knowledge shows up in every unit we build.

Our Core Technology

Zero-Leakage Magnetic Drive

Conventional reactors transmit torque through the vessel wall with a dynamic shaft seal (packing or mechanical seal) — a wear item, and a potential leak path. Our magnetic coupling drive transfers torque across the vessel wall with no shaft penetration, no dynamic seal, and no leakage path. That is why customers in harsh-duty processes specify magnetic-coupled chemical reactors.

🔥

Flammable & Explosive Media

Safe operation in hydrogenation and other high-temperature, high-pressure flammable services — with no leak path for the medium to escape through.

☣️

Toxic & Fine-Dust Media

Reliable service with highly toxic or fine-dust chemicals at high temperature and pressure. Zero seal-related emissions.

⏱️

Less Downtime

No packing to replace, no mechanical seal to maintain. Fewer shutdowns, lower lifetime cost for your plant.

Quality & Certifications

What We Won't Compromise On

ISO 9001 certified. Licensed Class I & II pressure vessel manufacturer since 2013. ASME product certification available on request. Every claim below is enforced on the production floor — not just in the manual.

📋

Material Certificates Are Not Optional

Every steel plate, every forging, every flange has a mill test certificate. We verify the grade, the chemistry, the mechanical properties. If a certificate is missing or doesn't match, the material doesn't enter production. Period.

🔍

NDT Reports Are Generated, Not Assumed

We don't say "NDT performed" and move on. Every weld joint that requires inspection is inspected. RT, UT, PT, MT — whatever the code and your service conditions require. Reports are generated, numbered, and filed with your documentation package.

💧

Hydrostatic Testing Is 100%, Not Sampling

Every pressure vessel is hydrostatically tested at 1.5× design pressure. No exemptions. No shortcuts. This isn't expensive — it's insurance. For you and for us.

🔒

Magnetic Coupling Seals on Every Reactor

Zero leakage is not a marketing claim — it's our standard. Packing seals leak. Mechanical seals wear. Magnetic coupling seals don't. If your medium is toxic, flammable, or valuable, this matters. Every reactor we build has a magnetic coupling seal.

✏️

Drawings Match What's Built

No "substantially equivalent" substitutions. No "we used what was available." What's on the approved drawing is what's in the vessel. If a change is needed, we flag it, get your approval, and update the drawing. You should always know exactly what you're getting.

📦

Proper Packaging and Lashing

A reactor that arrives damaged is a reactor that causes delays. We package for sea freight. Proper lashing. Moisture protection. Corner guards. We've seen what happens when suppliers cut corners on packaging — don't let that be your problem.
