6.1 Quality Standards

All reactors are manufactured in accordance with ASME Section VIII Division 1 (or your specified code: PED, GB150, EN13445). Materials comply with the grades specified in the contract. All welds are inspected according to the NDT plan agreed in the Technical Agreement.

6.2 Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)

You have the right to conduct or witness a FAT at our facility before shipment. The FAT includes hydrostatic testing, dimensional verification, and review of all quality documents. If you cannot attend in person, we can arrange a video call for remote witnessing.

6.3 Third-Party Inspection

Third-party inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Lloyd's Register is available at your expense. We will cooperate fully with the inspector and provide all necessary access and documentation.

7.1 Warranty Period

7.2 What Is Covered

  • Manufacturing defects (weld cracks, dimensional deviations beyond tolerance, inadequate pressure test)
  • Component failures under normal operating conditions (agitator shaft, bearing housing, seal housing — excluding consumable seals)

7.3 What Is Not Covered

  • Normal wear and tear (seal faces, gaskets, bearings under regular use)
  • Damage caused by improper installation, operation, or maintenance by the buyer

  • Damage caused by operating above the rated pressure or temperature
  • Modifications made by the buyer or third parties after delivery
