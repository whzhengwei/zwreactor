Industrial Production Reactors

Our industrial reactors handle everything from fine chemical synthesis to bulk pharmaceutical intermediates. We size heat transfer area based on your reaction kinetics — not a rule of thumb. If your cycle time is dominated by heating or cooling, we calculate whether your jacket is enough or whether you need internal coils.

Every reactor leaves with complete documentation: material certificates for every plate and forging, NDT reports, hydrostatic test certificate, nameplate data. If you're working with corrosive media, we flag material selection early — 304 versus 316L versus duplex versus lined. A wrong choice costs you months of unplanned downtime.
