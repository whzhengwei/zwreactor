Corrosion-Resistant Reactor Systems

When standard stainless steel won't withstand your process media, our non-ferrous metal reactors provide the corrosion resistance you need. We work with Hastelloy (C276, B2), Titanium (TA1, TA2, TA10), Monel 400, and Inconel 600/625 — all with full material certification and traceability.

Every alloy requires specialized welding procedures, argon backing gas protection, and post-weld heat treatment. We don't just weld exotic metals — we understand the metallurgy behind them. That knowledge prevents cracks, sensitization, and unexpected failures in service.

Material Selection

Choose the Right Alloy

Wrong material selection is the #1 cause of premature reactor failure. Tell us your medium, concentration, temperature, and we'll recommend the optimal material.

🛡️

Hastelloy C276

Excellent resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking. Ideal for oxidizing and reducing acid environments. The go-to choice for aggressive chemical processes.

⚡

Titanium TA2

Outstanding performance in oxidizing acids, seawater, and chlorine-containing media. Lightweight with excellent strength-to-weight ratio.

🔧

Monel 400

Excellent resistance to hydrofluoric acid, alkaline solutions, and seawater. Commonly used in fluoride processing and marine environments.

🌡️

Inconel 625

Superior high-temperature strength and oxidation resistance. Ideal for processes requiring both corrosion resistance and elevated temperature performance.

Quality Assurance

Specialized Welding for Exotic Alloys

Welding Hastelloy, titanium, and other exotic metals requires specialized procedures. Our welders are qualified per ASME Section IX, and every weld is inspected.

Procedure

Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS) developed and qualified for each alloy type before production begins.

Protection

Argon backing gas protection during welding to prevent oxidation and ensure weld integrity.

Heat Treatment

Post-weld heat treatment where required to restore corrosion resistance and relieve stresses.

NDT Inspection

Every weld joint inspected by RT, UT, PT, or MT as required by code and service conditions.

Passivation

Pickling and passivation treatment to restore the protective oxide layer on stainless and nickel alloys.

Documentation

Complete weld records including WPS, PQR, welder qualifications, and NDT reports.
