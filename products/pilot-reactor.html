Pilot Scale Reactor Systems

Our pilot reactors bridge the gap between lab R&D and full production. They are geometrically similar to production units, so data from pilot testing translates directly to scale-up decisions.

No shortcuts here — same magnetic coupling seals, same NDT procedures, same material traceability as our production reactors. When you run a pilot test, you need confidence that the results are real, not artifacts of inferior construction.
