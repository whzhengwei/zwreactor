Lift & Flip Reactor

Quick-opening batch reactor with full interior access — designed for processes requiring frequent cleaning, catalyst loading, or material inspection.

Home > Products > Lift & Flip Reactor

When a Standard Reactor Isn't Enough

Some processes require frequent access — loading catalyst pellets, inspecting residue buildup, cleaning between batches of different products. With a standard fixed-head reactor, that means dismantling bolts, removing the lid by crane, and dealing with gasket replacement every time. For high-frequency batch operations, that downtime adds up.

Our lift and flip reactors solve this. The entire vessel tilts on a hinge, opening the interior for full access without any bolt removal or disassembly. Pneumatic or hydraulic assist makes even large vessels easy to operate.

Volume Range100L – 5,000L
Design PressureUp to 10 MPa
Opening AngleUp to 120°
Drive OptionsPneumatic / hydraulic / manual
  • ✓ Quick-opening lid — no bolt removal required
  • ✓ Full interior access for cleaning and inspection
  • ✓ Magnetic coupling seal — zero leakage during operation
  • ✓ Safety interlocks prevent operation when open
  • ✓ Suitable for viscous materials and slurry processes
  • ✓ Reduces batch changeover time significantly
