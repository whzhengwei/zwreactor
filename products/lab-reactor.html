Laboratory Research Reactors

Our lab reactors are designed for academic and industrial R&D applications. Compact footprint, full magnetic coupling seal, and complete instrumentation make these units ideal for research labs, universities, and process development.

Every lab reactor is built to the same quality standards as our production units — same material certificates, same NDT procedures, same hydrostatic testing. We don't treat R&D equipment as "less important."
