A complete production line for unsaturated polyester resin

The Challenge

Unsaturated polyester resin equipment is the key equipment used to produce unsaturated polyester resin, phenolic resin, epoxy resin, ABS resin and paint. Resin production of this kind is a multi-step process, not a single reaction, and each step has its own temperature, pressure and hold time.

That is what makes it a systems problem rather than a vessel problem. The reaction itself has to be controlled precisely, but the vapour generated has to be taken overhead, fractionated, condensed and returned. Missing any one of those stages does not just reduce yield — it stops the line.

The customer needed a complete production line rather than a single reactor, and needed all of it in a material suitable for contact with the process.

Our Solution

We supplied a complete unsaturated polyester resin production system, built from the reaction pot through to the dilution side. The full set comprises:

  • ✓ Reaction pot (反应锅) — the primary reaction vessel
  • ✓ Vertical fractionating column (竖式分馏柱) — overhead separation of volatiles by boiling point
  • ✓ Horizontal condenser (卧式冷凝器) — condensing the overhead vapour stream
  • ✓ Water reservoir (贮水器) — process water management
  • ✓ Oil spill / overflow trough (溢油槽) — drainage and spill handling
  • ✓ Piping including the dilution kettle connection (对稀釜管线)

All parts of the equipment that come into contact with the process material are made of stainless steel. That covers the reaction pot, the fractionating column, the condenser and the associated piping — the whole wetted path, not only the main vessel.

Because the same equipment base serves phenolic, epoxy and ABS resin as well as polyester, the system was specified to be transferable across those products rather than dedicated to a single formulation.

System Configuration

  • ✓ Complete line from reaction through fractionation and condensation
  • ✓ All material-contacting parts in stainless steel
  • ✓ Vertical fractionating column for overhead product separation
  • ✓ Horizontal condenser with condensate return
  • ✓ Dilution kettle piping included
  • ✓ Applicable to unsaturated polyester, phenolic, epoxy and ABS resin, and paint
