Food Grade Reactor
5,000L • 304 Stainless Steel • Food Grade

The Challenge

A soy sauce manufacturer in Japan wanted to modernize their fermentation process. Their traditional wooden vessels produced good quality product but had inconsistent batch-to-batch results and required extensive manual cleaning. They needed a stainless steel reactor that could maintain precise temperature control during the 6-month fermentation cycle while meeting food safety standards.

The key requirements were:

  • ✓ FDA-compliant materials (304 stainless steel)
  • ✓ Smooth internal surfaces for easy cleaning
  • ✓ Temperature control: 25°C to 60°C (fermentation range)
  • ✓ Gentle agitation to support microbial activity
  • ✓ Sanitary connections throughout

Our Solution

We designed a 5,000L food-grade reactor with features optimized for fermentation processes:

Technical Specifications

Volume5,000L
Material304 Stainless Steel
Surface FinishRa ≤0.8μm (mechanical polished)
Design PressureAtmospheric (vented)
Temperature25°C to 60°C
AgitatorLow-shear turbine

Key Design Features

  • ✓ Dome-shaped head for complete drainage
  • ✓ Large manway (600mm) for cleaning and inspection
  • ✓ Semi-coil jacket for even temperature distribution
  • ✓ Low-shear paddle agitator — gentle mixing for microbial health
  • ✓ Sanitary tri-clamp connections (no dead legs)
  • ✓ CIP spray ball for automated cleaning
  • ✓ Fermentation air inlet with 0.2μm filter
  • ✓ Temperature probes at multiple heights

Why Food Grade Matters

Food-grade reactors have stricter requirements than industrial reactors:

  • ✓ Material certification: Every steel plate must have FDA-compliant mill certificates
  • ✓ Surface finish: Welds must be ground and polished to prevent bacterial harborage
  • ✓ Dead legs: No piping configurations that trap product — all connections must drain completely
  • ✓ Cleaning: CIP capability reduces labor and ensures consistent sanitation
  • ✓ Documentation: Full material traceability for food safety audits
