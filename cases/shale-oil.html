Shale Oil Refining — Multi-Layer Coil Reactor

Solving exothermic reaction heat removal with calculated coil design

Petrochemical

📍 Customer: Shale oil refining company, Southeast Asia

Home > Cases > Shale Oil Refining

The Challenge

The customer was processing shale oil through catalytic hydrogenation. The reaction is highly exothermic — it generates heat faster than a standard jacketed reactor can remove it. Their previous setup used a single-layer jacket, and they were seeing temperature spikes during the reaction peak. Risk of thermal runaway was real.

Our Solution

We designed a 6-layer internal cooling coil arrangement. Each layer was spaced to maximize heat transfer surface area while maintaining proper flow distribution. We calculated the required heat removal capacity based on their reaction kinetics data, then sized the coil layout accordingly.

The agitator was also customized — a hydrogenation-specific impeller that promotes gas-liquid mass transfer while keeping the cooling coils clear of dead zones.
