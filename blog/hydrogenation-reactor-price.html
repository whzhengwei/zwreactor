Hydrogenation Reactor Price Guide

Zhengwei Machinery provides competitive pricing for custom hydrogenation reactors. Our reactors are designed for high-pressure hydrogenation applications in pharmaceutical and chemical industries.

Price Factors

The final price of a hydrogenation reactor depends on several factors:

  • Capacity: 1L to 10,000L
  • Pressure: Up to 35 MPa
  • Materials: 316L, Titanium, Hastelloy
  • Features: Magnetic coupling, double seals, heating/cooling
  • Customization: Special requirements and specifications
