Why Choose Us

A Reactor Is a Production Tool. It Needs to Work.

Most reactor suppliers quote you on price. We ask about your medium, your temperature range, your cleaning schedule, your planned lifespan. Because a reactor that fails at month 8 costs you ten times what you saved on the purchase price.

⚙️

We Ask About Your Process First

Before quoting, we need to know: what's in the vessel? Is it corrosive? Does it degrade at high temperature? How often do you clean it? What's your batch cycle time target? A wrong material choice or inadequate heat transfer area shows up as downtime — not at purchase, but months later.

🔒

Zero Leakage Is Non-Negotiable

We use magnetic coupling seals on every reactor. No packing seals. No shaft seals. If your medium is toxic, flammable, or expensive, a leaking seal isn't an inconvenience — it's a shutdown. Our seals are maintenance-free and rated for your pressure and temperature.

Material certificates (MTR) for every plate and forging. NDT reports — RT, UT, PT, MT as applicable. Hydrostatic test report. Air tightness test. Nameplate data. Certificate of conformity. Nothing missing. Nothing generic. Your documents should match the reactor you receive.

Built for Export, Shipped Worldwide

ASME Section VIII compliant. GB150 certified. We handle packaging, lashing, and shipping documentation. Different countries have different tolerances for "roughly" — we don't play that game. What's on the drawing is what arrives.

About WEIHAI ZHENGWEI MACHINERY CO.,LTD

We Build Reactors. Not Displays.

WEIHAI ZHENGWEI MACHINERY CO.,LTD was founded in 2004. For over 20 years, we've been designing and manufacturing magnetic coupling reactors and complete chemical process equipment.

Our focus is narrow: reactors and pressure vessels. We don't diversify. We don't outsource the critical steps. We control material inspection, welding procedure qualification, NDT, pressure testing, and final inspection ourselves. This means we catch problems before they leave our yard.

In 2013, we obtained Type I and Type II Pressure Vessel Manufacturing License. Since then, every reactor leaves with full documentation: material certificates, NDT reports, pressure test certificates, and a conformity statement. What you order is what you receive. No surprises.

How We Work

From Your Process Conditions to a Running Reactor

We don't start with a catalog. We start with your medium, your temperature, your pressure, your batch cycle time target. Everything else follows.

01

Share Your Process Conditions

Volume, medium composition, operating temperature and pressure, agitation requirement, cleaning frequency, expected lifespan. The more detail you give, the better we can engineer your reactor.

02

We Review and Advise

Material selection check. Heat transfer area calculation. Agitator type recommendation. Sealing solution. We flag issues before you pay — wrong material, insufficient cooling, awkward nozzle placement. You decide what to change.

03

Drawing and Quotation

Detailed engineering drawings. Technical specification document. Formal quotation with itemized pricing. No hidden costs. Delivery timeline confirmed.

04

Manufacturing and Inspection

Material incoming inspection. Welding procedure qualification. Process control at each stage. NDT as required. Hydrostatic test. Final inspection. You can visit our facility or request third-party inspection (SGS, BV, etc.).

05

Documentation and Shipping

Complete documentation package: MTRs, NDT reports, pressure test certificates, nameplate data, conformity certificate, packing list. Sea-worthy packaging. Proper lashing. We track your reactor until it arrives.

06

After-Sales Support

Installation guidance. Commissioning support if needed. Spare parts available. We respond to technical questions within 24 hours. A reactor that's running well is the best advertisement we have.
