Our Services

Full-Spectrum Reactor Services

From your first process inquiry to years of production — engineering design, quality-managed manufacturing, spare parts, training, and on-site support. One team, every step of the way.

A reactor doesn't arrive at your site and disappear into operation. It needs a design that matches your process, manufacturing that meets your quality standards, documentation your auditors will accept, spare parts when something wears out, and people who can troubleshoot at 2 AM your time. We cover all of it — not through partners, but through our own team.

Service Lines

What We Do For You

01

Engineering & Design Review

We review your process conditions before we cut steel. Material selection check. Heat transfer area calculation. Agitator type recommendation. Sealing solution evaluation. Nozzle layout optimization. We flag issues you may not have considered — and you decide what to change.

  • Process condition review (medium, temp, pressure, cycle time)
  • Material compatibility assessment
  • Heat transfer area calculation (jacket vs. coil)
  • Agitator type and power selection
  • Nozzle orientation and instrument layout
  • Pressure vessel code compliance check
Request a design review →
02

Manufacturing & Quality

Quality is designed in, not inspected in. Every batch of steel plate is verified. Every weld has a qualified procedure and a qualified welder. Every NDT report is traceable. Every reactor is hydrostatic tested before it leaves the yard.

  • Material incoming inspection (MTR verification)
  • Welding procedure qualification (WPS/PQR)
  • NDT: RT, UT, PT, MT as required by code
  • Hydrostatic test and air tightness test
  • Interior/exterior polishing to specification
  • Final dimensional verification

  • 24-hour technical response (zwfw@whzhengweihj.com)

Operator Training

A well-trained operator catches problems before they become failures. We provide operation manuals, maintenance guides, and hands-on training — either at our facility or on-site at your plant. Your team should know how the reactor works, not just how to turn it on.

How It Works

From Inquiry to Production

1

Process Inquiry & Design Review

You send your process conditions. We review them — material, heat transfer, agitation, sealing, nozzle layout. We flag issues and recommend improvements. You approve the design or request changes. No cost for the review.

2

Quotation & Contract

FAQ

Service Questions

Do I need to visit your factory before ordering?

No. Many of our customers never visit — they review our certifications, request third-party inspection during manufacturing, and verify documentation upon delivery. But if you'd like to visit, we welcome you. You'll see the manufacturing process, meet the team, and see reactors being built for your industry.

How fast do you respond to technical questions?

Within 24 hours, usually much faster. Our after-sales team (zwfw@whzhengweihj.com) handles all post-sale technical inquiries. For urgent issues, WhatsApp or WeChat messages get a response the same day.

Can you send an engineer to my site?

Yes. For installation supervision, commissioning support, or troubleshooting, we can send a qualified engineer to your location. Travel and accommodation costs are quoted upfront — no hidden fees. This is common for turnkey projects and complex systems.

What spare parts do you keep in stock?

Common consumables: mechanical seals (various sizes), agitator components (impellers, shaft seals), gauge glass assemblies, thermowell elements, safety valve replacements, gasket sets. Full spare parts list is provided with every order. Additional parts can be ordered within 2–4 weeks.

Do you offer operator training?

Yes. We provide operation manuals, maintenance guides, and hands-on training. Training can be conducted at our facility in Weihai or on-site at your plant. Materials are provided in English or your preferred language.

What if I need modifications after delivery?

Modifications are common — different product, different medium, expanded capacity. We engineer the modification, manufacture the parts, and provide installation guidance. Every modification is documented so your reactor records stay current.
