Quality Assurance

Certified Manufacturing Standards

Quality isn't something we inspect in at the end — it's designed, manufactured, and managed into every reactor we build. These certifications are the framework that makes that possible.

🏆

Pressure Vessel Manufacturing License

Type I & Type II — Approved since 2013. This is not a general equipment license — it's specific authorization to manufacture pressure vessels under Chinese national standards (GB150), with rigorous oversight on design, materials, welding, and testing.

✓

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management

Complete quality management system covering design, procurement, manufacturing, inspection, and delivery. Not just a certificate on the wall — it means documented procedures, corrective action processes, and continuous improvement cycles.

🔬

Material Traceability

Every steel plate, forging, flange, and welding consumable comes with certified mill test reports and batch traceability. We verify incoming materials against your specification — no substitutions without your approval.

🔍

NDT Testing Coverage

Radiographic (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Penetrant (PT), and Magnetic Particle (MT) testing per ASME and GB standards. Weld joint inspection ratios are determined by service conditions — not minimum code requirements.

💧

Hydrostatic & Air Tightness Testing

Every pressure vessel undergoes hydrostatic test at 1.5× design pressure and air tightness testing before shipment. These aren't optional checks — they're the final verification that your reactor will hold pressure when it matters.

📋

Complete Documentation Package

Material certificates, NDT reports, pressure test reports, nameplate data, and certificate of conformity — all included with every shipment. No missing documents, no last-minute scrambles for your import clearance.

Quality Process

How We Ensure Quality

Quality is built in at every stage — from raw material incoming inspection to final shipping documentation.

1

Incoming Material Inspection

Every batch of steel plate, forging, flange, and seal undergoes material certificate verification and re-inspection. Chemical composition, mechanical properties, and dimensional tolerance are checked against your specification before materials enter production.

2

Process Control

Cutting accuracy, rolling roundness, welding procedure qualification, welder certification, weld appearance — every process step has documented control points. Welders are certified for specific positions and procedures. No unauthorized substitutions.

3

NDT Testing

RT/UT/PT/MT testing per code requirements. Weld joint inspection ratios are determined by service conditions — high-pressure, toxic, or flammable media get higher inspection coverage. Full inspection reports provided with documentation package.

4

Final Inspection & Testing

Hydrostatic test, air tightness test, polishing roughness verification, internal cleanliness, paint adhesion testing — every reactor leaves our facility only after passing all final inspection criteria. Documentation completeness is verified before packing.
