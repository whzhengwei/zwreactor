## Content Summary

The article covers 7 common batch reactor design defects:

1. **Material Selection** - 304 SS fails with chlorides >60°C
2. **Agitator Mismatch** - Frame agitator doesn't handle high viscosity
3. **Insufficient Cooling** - Jacket often 30-40% undersized
4. **Wrong Seal Type** - $200 packing seal vs $50K shutdown cost
5. **Under-Rated Pressure** - Nameplate ≠ design pressure
6. **Poor Nozzle Layout** - Installation nightmare on site
7. **Incomplete Instruments** - Blind spots cause batch rejections

Plus a scale-up warning section and a pre-sign checklist.
